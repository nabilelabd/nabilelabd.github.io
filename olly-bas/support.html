A quiz for a room full of people.

A category says it needs internet. Why?
Guess the Song, Movie Posters, Guess the Series and Guess Who Scored play or show media hosted by Apple, TMDB and YouTube. Everything else — including every photograph — is inside the app and works offline.
A song will not play.
Check the ring switch on the side of the phone. The app asks iOS to play in silent mode, but a phone in Focus or with Bluetooth connected elsewhere may send the sound somewhere you cannot hear it.
Can we get the same questions twice?
Not once you have seen the answer. Opening a question and backing out without revealing it leaves the question available.
How do I reset our progress?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it.
The charades QR code will not scan.
Point a second phone's camera at it. The word opens in that phone's browser so it never appears on the screen the room is watching.
Something is wrong with a question.
Email nabil.elabd@hotmail.com with the category and the question and it will be fixed in the next update.
