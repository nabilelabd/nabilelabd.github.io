Privacy Policy

Olly Bas · last updated 12 September 2026

Olly Bas does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking identifier.

What is stored, and where

The app remembers which questions this phone has already been asked, so that the next game deals you new ones. That record is a list of question identifiers. It is stored on the device itself and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network requests

Some categories show or play media that is hosted by its rights holder rather than bundled with the app. Playing those categories requests a file by URL. No information about you is sent with those requests, and the app has no user information to send.

  • audio-ssl.itunes.apple.com — Apple's own thirty-second song previews
  • image.tmdb.org — film and series poster images from TMDB
  • www.youtube.com — YouTube's embedded player, for one football category

Those services may log the request as any web server does. Their own privacy policies govern that, and we receive nothing back from them about you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, collects nothing, and shows no advertising.

Attribution

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licences and are credited inside the app, on the credits screen at the end of the category list. Posters come from TMDB.
