Privacy Policy

Olly Bas · last updated 13 November 2026

Olly Bas does not collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone about you is the record of a purchase, if you make one, and that record has no name, email or contact details in it.

What is stored on your phone

The app remembers which questions this phone has already been asked, so that the next game deals you new ones, along with your settings (theme, language, timer) and how many free games you have played in each mode. These stay on the device. Deleting the app deletes the question record and settings; the free-game count and whether you have unlocked the game are kept in the phone's secure keychain so that reinstalling does not reset them.

The optional unlock

Olly Bas gives you free games in every mode and offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase that unlocks everything. Players who had the app before the purchase existed keep everything free.

  • Apple handles the payment. We never see your card, your Apple ID or your name.
  • RevenueCat checks the purchase. To know whether this phone has bought the unlock, and to restore it on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives a random ID the app creates (not linked to you) and the purchase record Apple provides: what was bought, when, the price and the store country. It is used only to make the unlock work, never for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

1 v 1 online

When two phones play 1 v 1 with a room code, the moves pass between them through Supabase Realtime (supabase.co): the room code, the team names you type and which answers are picked. Nothing is saved on a server; the room disappears when the game ends.

Media from other services

Some categories show or play media hosted by its rights holder rather than bundled with the app. Playing them requests a file by URL, with no information about you.

  • audio-ssl.itunes.apple.com — Apple's own thirty-second song previews
  • image.tmdb.org — film and series images from TMDB
  • www.youtube.com — YouTube's embedded player, for video clips

Those services may log the request as any web server does. Their own privacy policies govern that, and we receive nothing back from them about you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and shows no advertising. Purchases go through Apple, where parents can require approval with Ask to Buy.
